# Let's Truck Nutritional Guidelines
## The NDK (Nutrient Dense Keto) Framework

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** January 6, 2026
**Author:** Kevin Rutherford

---

## Core Philosophy

We recommend a **Paleo-based, animal-heavy diet** optimized for metabolic health. This is NOT mainstream nutrition advice - it's based on ancestral eating patterns and designed specifically for drivers dealing with blood sugar issues, inflammation, and metabolic dysfunction.

**The NDK Protocol:**
- **N**utrient **D**ense **K**eto
- Animal-based, high-fat, very low carb
- Focus on nutrient density per calorie, not calorie restriction

|-------|--------|-------|

## APPROVED FOODS

### Tier 1: Prioritize These (Eat Freely)
**Animal Proteins:**
- Beef (especially grass-fed) - ribeye, ground beef, roasts
- Lamb
- Pork - bacon, pork belly, chops
- Eggs (whole, don't fear the yolk)
- Organ meats (liver, heart) - most nutrient-dense foods on earth
- Bone broth

**Fats:**
- Beef tallow
- Lard/bacon grease
- Butter (grass-fed preferred)
- Ghee
- Duck fat

**Seafood:**
- Wild-caught salmon
- Sardines
- Mackerel
- Shrimp
- Oysters (zinc powerhouse)

### Tier 2: Good Options
**Dairy (Quality Matters):**
- Raw A2 dairy (ideal but hard to find)
- **Alexandre Farms A2** - best practical option for drivers
- Full-fat cheese (aged is better)
- Heavy cream
- Sour cream
- Avoid: conventional milk, low-fat dairy, A1 dairy

**Poultry:**
- Chicken thighs (dark meat preferred over breast)
- Turkey
- Duck

**Other Fats:**
- Avocado oil (for cooking)
- Olive oil (cold use only)
- Coconut oil
- MCT oil

### Tier 3: Limited/Occasional
**Low-Carb Vegetables (VERY LIMITED):**
- Avoid most vegetables due to phytates, oxalates, lectins
- If eating veggies, stick to:
  - Zucchini
  - Cucumber
  - Small amounts of leafy greens (well-cooked)
- Always cook vegetables to reduce anti-nutrients
- Vegetables are NOT required for health

**Fruit (Only When Metabolically Healthy):**
- Avoid fruit entirely during metabolic healing phase
- Once A1C is optimal and insulin sensitive:
  - Small amounts of berries only
  - Occasional avocado (technically a fruit)
- No tropical fruits, no juice, no dried fruit

**Nuts (Very Limited):**
- Macadamia nuts (lowest in omega-6)
- Avoid: almonds, cashews, peanuts (high oxalates/lectins)

## FOODS TO AVOID

### Never Eat
- Seed oils (soybean, canola, corn, vegetable, sunflower, safflower)
- Sugar in all forms
- Grains (wheat, corn, rice, oats)
- Bread, pasta, cereal
- Legumes (beans, lentils, peanuts)
- Soy products
- Processed foods
- Fast food (with rare exceptions noted below)

### Avoid Until Metabolically Healthy
- All fruit
- Most vegetables
- All starchy foods
- Anything that spikes blood sugar

### Problematic "Health Foods"
- Whole grains (still grains)
- Oatmeal (high carb, contains anti-nutrients)
- Fruit juice (liquid sugar)
- Smoothies (usually sugar bombs)
- Low-fat anything (usually higher sugar)
- Plant-based meat alternatives
- Most protein bars (candy bars in disguise)

## TRUCK STOP SURVIVAL GUIDE (NDK Protocol)

### Pilot/Flying J
**Best Options:**
- Hard boiled eggs (grab several)
- Pork rinds (zero carb, good fat)
- Cheese sticks / string cheese
- Deli meat (check for no sugar added)
- Beef jerky (check ingredients - avoid sugar/soy)
- Hot dogs (no bun) - not ideal but acceptable

**Avoid:**
- Salads (dressing has seed oils, croutons)
- Anything breaded
- Sandwiches
- All pastries/donuts

### Love's
**Best Options:**
- Hardee's/Carl's Jr: Order any burger, no bun, no sauce
- Godfather's Pizza: Scrape toppings off, eat meat and cheese only
- Chester's chicken: Remove breading, eat the meat
- Grab-and-go: eggs, cheese, pork rinds

### TA/Petro (Iron Skillet)
**Best Options:**
- Eggs any style with bacon/sausage
- Steak (no sides, or sub for extra meat)
- Burger patties (no bun)
- Breakfast meats

**Ask for:**
- Extra butter
- Meat instead of hash browns
- No bread/toast

### Fast Food (Emergency Options)
**McDonald's:**
- Sausage patties (order just the meat)
- Burger patties no bun

**Wendy's:**
- Baconator no bun
- Any burger, no bun, no sauce

**Five Guys:**
- Burger bowl (no bun)
- Extra bacon, extra cheese

## THE 7-DAY NDK RESET PROTOCOL

### Day 1-3: Elimination
- Eat ONLY: beef, eggs, salt, water
- No dairy, no spices (temporarily)
- This identifies any sensitivities
- Expect: possible fatigue, headaches (carb withdrawal)

### Day 4-5: Reintroduce Fats
- Add: butter, bacon, pork belly
- Continue: beef, eggs
- Increase salt intake (important!)
- Drink bone broth for electrolytes

### Day 6-7: Expand Proteins
- Add: other meats, seafood
- Add: quality dairy (A2 if possible)
- Add: cheese
- By now: energy should be stabilizing

### Week 2+: Maintenance
- Stay in NDK framework
- 70-80% fat, minimal carbs
- Occasional Tier 3 foods if tolerated
- No fruit until A1C < 5.4 and fasting insulin optimal

## SUPPLEMENTS THAT WORK

### Recommended
- **Electrolytes** (critical on keto): sodium, potassium, magnesium
- **Beef liver capsules** (if not eating organ meats)
- **Vitamin D3 + K2** (especially for drivers with limited sun)
- **Omega-3 fish oil** (high quality only)
- **Magnesium glycinate** (sleep, cramps, mood)

### For Blood Sugar Specifically
- **Berberine** - powerful for glucose control
- **Chromium** - supports insulin sensitivity
- **Apple cider vinegar** (before meals) - blunts glucose spike

### Avoid
- Multivitamins (most are garbage)
- Synthetic B vitamins
- Calcium supplements (get from food/dairy)
- Fiber supplements (not needed on animal-based diet)

## COMMON OBJECTIONS HANDLED

**"But I need fiber for digestion"**
→ Fiber is not essential. Many people digest BETTER without it. Animal foods are highly digestible. If constipated, it's usually an electrolyte issue, not fiber.

**"Red meat causes heart disease"**
→ Outdated hypothesis based on flawed studies. Saturated fat and cholesterol from whole foods do not cause heart disease. Seed oils and sugar do.

**"This is too expensive"**
→ Ground beef and eggs are cheap. You eat less volume when eating nutrient-dense food. You save money on snacks, energy drinks, and medical bills.

**"I can't find good food on the road"**
→ Every truck stop has eggs, pork rinds, and cheese. Fast food can be hacked (burgers no bun). Bring a cooler with ground beef and pre-cooked meat.

**"I'll miss bread/pasta/sweets"**
→ Cravings disappear after 2-3 weeks of strict adherence. Your taste buds literally change. Former carb addicts become disgusted by their old foods.
